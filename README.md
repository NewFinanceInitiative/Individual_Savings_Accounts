# Instant Savings Accounts (Smart Contract Based ISA's)

Smart contract based ISA's for Ethereum.

The smart contracts in this archive are developed in solidity and are EVM based code scripts that operate as instant savings accounts (ISA's)for the blockchain and EVM digital currencies. The incentive to produce these products is aimed at creating more consumables for the decentralised finance markets and enabling productive smart contracts to work for individuals and market participants with supporting the provision of programmability with digital currencies and giving more ways to consume blockchain technology infrastructure. 

Smart contract based ISA's operate as individual savings vehicles for blockchain based digital currencies. The application of the smart contract based ISA's range from the simple traditional functionality of savings per year or for a specific amount of time, as a pension deposit account, or 5-10 year savings accumulator. The blockchain allows for more dynamic ISA's to be created because of the instance of programmability. The difference in ISA's that come from using blockchain based smart contracts range from having different deposit techniques and withdrawal thresholds that can be dynamic with allowing consumers to access funds and remain as savers or investors in the markets.
